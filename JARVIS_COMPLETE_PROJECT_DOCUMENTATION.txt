1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE
====================================================================================================
HM AI 4.0 (JARVIS) is a state-of-the-art, institutional-grade autonomous quantitative trading system
engineered for high-precision algorithmic execution across global markets: Forex, Commodities (Gold/Oil),
Cryptocurrencies, US Equities, and Indian Equities / F&O Options.

The architecture is built upon four fundamental pillars:
  1. Multi-Agent Parallel Intelligence: Independent domain analysts execute in parallel threads to
     evaluate Market Structure, Momentum, Liquidity, Volatility, Macroeconomics, and Portfolio Risk,
     with an adversarial Devil's Advocate agent challenging every prospective signal.
  2. Proven 100-Year Market Confluence: Synthesizes Smart Money Concepts (SMC), Inner Circle Trader (ICT),
     Wyckoff Accumulation/Distribution phases, and Mark Minervini SEPA / Volatility Contraction Patterns (VCP).
  3. Continuous Self-Learning & Online Machine Learning: Employs Online Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)
     with L2 Regularization, Multi-Armed Bandits (LinUCB + Thompson Sampling + EXP3), Fractional
     Differentiation feature transformers, and Meta-Labelers to dynamically adapt to evolving market regimes.
  4. Multi-Layered Capital Preservation: Enforces fractional Kelly criterion position sizing, 15-gate
     adaptive same-symbol risk filtering, strict equity tier constraints, hardware-level emergency stop
     guards, and consecutive loss cooldown mechanisms.

3. AGENTS, SUBAGENTS & PARALLEL ANALYST CLUSTER
====================================================================================================
HM AI 4.0 utilizes a multi-agent distributed intelligence model. Execution is divided among
specialized autonomous agents and a parallel cluster of domain experts.

3.1 Master System Agents:
  - `master-trader` (JarvisOrchestrator): Coordinates market sweeps, initiates parallel analysis,
    validates risk constraints, reconciles MT5 broker positions, and manages trade execution.
  - `code-writer`: Responsible for runtime code modification, automated hotfixes, and refactoring
    within the trading environment without breaking architectural invariants.
  - `research`: Ingests market whitepapers, backtest datasets, and empirical historical results to
    tune hyper-parameters and update strategy rules.
  - `self` (Supervisor & Watchdog): Monitors thread liveness, cleans up stale execution locks, checks
    MT5 socket connectivity, and enforces emergency fail-safe shutdowns.

3.2 Parallel Analyst Cluster (`jarvis/analysts/parallel_runner.py`):
Dispatches 7 specialized domain analysts concurrently in parallel threads using `ThreadPoolExecutor(max_workers=8)`
with a strict 2.0-second timeout ceiling via `TimeoutGuard`.

1. Structure Analyst (`StructureAnalyst`):
   - Evaluates higher timeframe (HTF) market structure: Swing Highs, Swing Lows, Break of Structure (BOS),
     and Change of Character (CHoCH).
   - Generates structural bias (BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL) and identifies key support/resistance zones.

2. Momentum Analyst (`MomentumAnalyst`):
   - Analyzes directional momentum using ADX (Average Directional Index), DI+ / DI- spread, RSI divergence,
     and EMA 20/50/200 slope alignment.
   - Generates a continuous Trend Score (-100 to +100).

3. Liquidity Analyst (`LiquidityAnalyst`):
   - Identifies buy-side liquidity (BSL) and sell-side liquidity (SSL) pools, equal highs/lows (EQH/EQL),
     and institutional stop hunts (liquidity sweeps).

4. Volatility Analyst (`VolatilityAnalyst`):
   - Measures ATR relative to historical median (ATR Ratio), Bollinger Band bandwidth, and Keltner Squeeze
     states (Compression vs Expansion).

5. Macro Analyst (`MacroAnalyst`):
   - Assesses macroeconomic backdrop: US Dollar Index (DXY) momentum, 10-Year Treasury Yields, and high-impact
     calendar events (FOMC, NFP, CPI) to prevent trading during liquidity vacuums.

6. Risk Analyst (`RiskAnalyst`):
   - Computes real-time portfolio heat, correlation clustering, currency directional exposure, and account
     margin utilization.

7. Devil's Advocate Analyst (`DevilAdvocateAnalyst`):
   - Acts as an adversarial challenger to the tentative trade bias.
   - Detects liquidity traps, counter-trend traps, exhaustion wicks, and excessive spread friction.
   - Generates an adversarial penalty score (0.0 to 35.0) that directly deducts from trade quality score.

4. MULTI-ASSET STRATEGIES & SMC / ICT / WYCKOFF FRAMEWORK
====================================================================================================
HM AI 4.0 integrates 100 years of proven market theory into a unified algorithmic framework:

4.1 Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & ICT Core Framework:
  - Fair Value Gaps (FVG): Detects 3-candle imbalance zones (Bullish FVG: Low of candle 3 > High of candle 1;
    Bearish FVG: High of candle 3 < Low of candle 1). Tracks premium/discount midpoint (CE - Consequent Encroachment).
  - Order Blocks (OB): Identifies the final opposing candle before a violent displacement that causes BOS.
  - Breaker Blocks: Identifies failed Order Blocks that transition into powerful opposing support/resistance.
  - Killzones: Enforces prime institutional liquidity sessions:
      * London Open Killzone: 07:00 - 10:00 UTC
      * New York Open Killzone: 12:00 - 15:00 UTC
      * London Close Killzone: 15:00 - 17:00 UTC
      * Asian Range Consolidation: 00:00 - 06:00 UTC
  - ICT Silver Bullet: High-probability 1-hour window (14:00 - 15:00 UTC) executing liquidity sweep + displacement + FVG retest.

4.2 Wyckoff Method Fusion:
  - Accumulation Phase: Preliminary Support (PS) -> Selling Climax (SC) -> Automatic Rally (AR) -> Secondary Test (ST)
    -> Phase C Spring (False breakdown liquidity sweep) -> Sign of Strength (SOS) -> Last Point of Support (LPS).
  - Distribution Phase: Preliminary Supply (PSY) -> Buying Climax (BC) -> Automatic Reaction (AR) -> Secondary Test (ST)
    -> Phase C Upthrust After Distribution (UTAD) -> Sign of Weakness (SOW) -> Last Point of Supply (LPSY).

4.3 Mark Minervini SEPA & Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP):
  - 8-Point Trend Template: Price > 50-day EMA > 150-day EMA > 200-day EMA, 200-day EMA trending upward for >= 1 month,
    price within 25% of 52-week high, and relative strength ranking >= 70.
  - VCP Contraction Waves: Detects progressive volume and price range contraction across consecutive swings
    (Wave 1: ~15-25% depth -> Wave 2: ~8-12% depth -> Wave 3: ~3-5% depth with drying volume before breakout).

4.4 Institutional Setup Tiers:
  - ELITE (Score >= 70 / Win Prob >= 68%): Full confluence (Triple Confluence: FVG + OB + Breaker + Prime Killzone).
  - HIGH (Score 55-69 / Win Prob 62-67%): Dual confluence with HTF alignment and positive EV.
  - MODERATE (Score 40-54 / Win Prob 55-61%): Valid setup with minor adversarial drag or lower timeframe trigger.
  - LOW / REJECTED (Score < 40 / Win Prob < 55%): Discarded by Quality Gate.

5. AI DECISION ENGINE, MASTER CONFLUENCE & DYNAMIC LEVELS ENGINE

5.1 Master Confluence Scorer (`MasterConfluenceEngine`):
Synthesizes multi-factor evidence into a composite 100-point confluence score:
  - Wyckoff + ICT Fusion (20 pts): Spring/UTAD detection + BOS/CHoCH alignment.
  - Minervini Trend Template (20 pts): Trend Score velocity + ADX strength.
  - Volatility Contraction Pattern (20 pts): 3-stage range compression or volatility compression proxy.
  - ICT Killzone & AMD Phase (20 pts): Execution inside active Killzone + displacement confirmation.
  - Triple Confluence (20 pts): Confluence of Fair Value Gap, Order Block, and Breaker Block.

5.2 Mathematical Dynamic Levels Engine (`DynamicRiskAndLevelsEngine`):
Eliminates rigid static tables in favor of continuous mathematical formulas:
  - Dynamic Volatility Buffer:
      $$\text{Buffer} = \text{ATR} \times \left( \alpha_{\text{base}} + \beta_{\text{vol}} \times \frac{\text{ATR}_{\text{cur}}}{\text{ATR}_{\text{median}}} + \gamma_{\text{spread}} \times \frac{\text{Spread}_{\text{cur}}}{\text{Spread}_{\text{typ}}} \right)$$
      where $\alpha_{\text{base}} = 0.12$, $\beta_{\text{vol}} = 0.05$, $\gamma_{\text{spread}} = 0.05$.
  - Dynamic Structural Stop Loss (SL):
      * For BUY: $\text{SL} = \min(\text{Swing Low}, \text{OB Low}) - \text{Dynamic Buffer}$
      * For SELL: $\text{SL} = \max(\text{Swing High}, \text{OB High}) + \text{Dynamic Buffer}$
  - Liquidity-Anchored Take Profit (TP):
      * Primary TP anchored to nearest opposing unmitigated Order Block / Liquidity Pool (target R:R 1.5 to 3.5+).
  - Adaptive Scale-Out Plan:
      * First Target (50-60% volume): Reached at +1.0R to +1.5R.
      * Breakeven Ratchet: Automatically moves SL to Entry + Buffer upon First Target fill.
      * Runner (40-50% volume): Trailed using 1.5x ATR dynamic trailing stop.

6. MACHINE LEARNING & REINFORCEMENT LEARNING SUITE
====================================================================================================
6.1 Online Adaptive Machine Learning Predictor (`OnlineMLPredictor`):
Real-time adaptive binary classifier predicting trade success probability $P(\text{Win})$.
  - Model: Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) Logistic Regression with $L_2$ Ridge Regularization.
  - Loss Function: Binary Cross-Entropy with $L_2$ Penalty:
      $$J(w) = -\left[ y \log \hat{y} + (1-y)\log(1-\hat{y}) \right] + \frac{\lambda}{2} \|w\|^2$$
  - 9-Dimensional Feature Vector:
      1. `momentum_trend_score`: Trend score (-1.0 to +1.0) aligned with trade direction.
      2. `adx_strength`: Normalized trend strength ($\text{ADX} - 15) / 35$.
      3. `volatility_atr_ratio`: Current ATR normalized against expected baseline.
      4. `rsi_distance`: RSI deviation from neutral 50.0.
      5. `spread_friction_ratio`: Spread-to-ATR friction cost (scale-invariant).
      6. `adversarial_penalty`: Devil's Advocate penalty normalized (0.0 to 1.0).
      7. `structure_alignment`: BOS/CHoCH structural alignment (+1.0 Bull, -1.0 Bear).
      8. `session_prime_flag`: Binary bonus for Prime London/NY sessions.
      9. `mtf_confluence_score`: Macro D1/H4/H1 directional alignment score.
  - Bayesian Prior: Initialized with $\approx 55\%$ baseline win rate and institutional feature priors.
  - Persistence: Model weights and biases automatically saved to `jarvis_online_ml_weights.json`.

6.2 Multi-Armed Bandit Strategy Allocator (`EnsembleStrategyBandit`):
Dynamically re-weights strategy allocations using an ensemble of three reinforcement learning algorithms:
  - Thompson Sampling: Samples from Beta distribution $\text{Beta}(\alpha + 1, \beta + 1)$ for each arm.
  - Upper Confidence Bound (LinUCB / UCB1): $\text{UCB}_i = \hat{\mu}_i + \sqrt{\frac{2 \ln N}{n_i}}$.
  - Exponential-Weight Exploration & Exploitation (EXP3): $\text{Weight}_i = \exp\left(\min(5.0, \frac{R_i}{n_i})\right)$.
  - Composite Selection Score: $0.4 \times \text{Thompson} + 0.4 \times \text{UCB} + 0.2 \times \text{EXP3}$.

6.3 Fractional Differentiation Transformer (`FractionalDifferentiationTransformer`):
Implements Marcos López de Prado's Fixed-Width Window Fractional Differentiation ($0 < d < 1$):
  - Solves the Fundamental Dilemma: Price differencing ($d=1$) achieves stationarity but erases all memory;
    raw prices ($d=0$) preserve memory but violate stationarity.
  - Binomial Series Expansion: $(1-B)^d = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (-1)^k \binom{d}{k} B^k$.
  - Generates stationary features (ADF test $p < 0.05$) while preserving $>80\%$ of long-range autocorrelation.

6.4 ML Meta-Labeler (`MetaLabeler`):
Secondary decision classifier based on Lopez de Prado's meta-labeling architecture:
  - Primary Engine determines direction (BUY / SELL).

    maximum drawdown from 64% to under 12%.
  - Drawdown Scaling: Risk scaled down as account approaches maximum allowable drawdown.
